# Asian Secret Website Design System Prompt

Create a complete design system and website visual direction for **Asian Secret / AsianSecret.bg**, a premium boutique for Japanese and Asian skincare, sheet masks, SPF, eye patches, hand care, hair care, and targeted beauty treatments.

Use the existing brand mark from `asian_secret_as_logo_transparent.png`: elegant black **AS** serif monogram, thin warm-gold circle details, soft cherry blossom elements. The system should feel refined, feminine, clean, trustworthy, and modern, with a light Japanese/K-beauty editorial mood rather than a loud marketplace look.

## Brand Mood

Design for a premium Asian cosmetics shop focused on:

- Natural ingredients
- Effective formulas
- Japanese technologies
- Daily skincare rituals
- Premium quality
- Hydration, brightening, protection, soothing, anti-aging, and skin barrier care

The main visual theme should be soft ivory, porcelain, blossom pink, botanical green, warm gold, and deep black. The design should also support different product-color stories so every product line can keep its own identity while still looking like one brand.

## Core Palette

Use these global brand colors:

- Porcelain Ivory: `#F8F2ED` for main backgrounds
- Soft Warm Cream: `#EFE3D7` for secondary panels and subtle section backgrounds
- Blossom Pink: `#F3A9AD` for feminine accents, hover states, and editorial moments
- Antique Gold: `#C89235` for dividers, icons, outline details, badges, and premium cues
- Ink Black: `#0A0908` for logo, headings, navigation, and strong contrast
- Muted Rose: `#B85D68` for campaign headings and selected highlights
- Botanical Green: `#7D9A5D` for natural/plant-based ranges
- Clean Aqua: `#68B9C7` for hydration products

## Product Accent Colors

Create product-category color tokens that can be used for cards, tags, filters, icons, and product collection pages:

- Bakuchiol / anti-aging: soft lavender and violet
- Horse oil / nourishment: amber, peach, and warm orange
- Hydration masks: aqua, sky blue, and glassy cyan
- Aomikan / citrus freshness: lemon yellow and fresh green
- Yomogi / botanical calming: matcha green and leaf green
- Propolis / repair: coral red and honey gold
- Premium Resbro / luxury masks: champagne, pearl beige, and metallic gold
- SPF / sun protection: rich violet and clean white
- Eye patches / brightening: soft pink and candy rose
- Natural cotton masks: sage green, milky white, and botanical neutrals
- Hair-care cream: black, nude beige, and soft champagne

Keep these accent colors controlled: use them as supporting product identity, not as full-page background colors. The overall brand shell should stay ivory, black, gold, and blossom pink.

## Typography

Use elegant, high-contrast typography for the brand:

- Headings: a refined serif with luxury beauty feeling, such as `Cormorant Garamond`, `Playfair Display`, or `Libre Baskerville`.
- Body/UI: a clean modern sans-serif with Cyrillic support, such as `Inter`, `Manrope`, or `Noto Sans`.
- Letterspacing should be generous only for short labels, nav items, and category eyebrow text.
- Support Bulgarian and Cyrillic text cleanly. Avoid fonts that do not render Cyrillic well.

## Layout System

Build the website around calm, high-end ecommerce structure:

- Header: white/ivory background, centered or left AS logo, clear categories, search, account, cart.
- Hero: first viewport should show the Asian Secret brand immediately with real product imagery or the AS logo mark. Use soft blossom/product photography, not abstract gradients.
- Product cards: clean grid, 8px or smaller radius, product image dominant, short benefit tags, price, stock state, wishlist, and quick add.
- Collection pages: left filter sidebar on desktop, slide-over filters on mobile, color-coded product benefits.
- Product detail pages: large image gallery, benefit badges, ingredient highlights, usage instructions, skin type, texture, size, and add-to-cart.
- Education sections: ingredient explainers, routine builder, mask finder, SPF guide, and "shop by skin concern".
- Checkout/cart: minimal, clear, trust-building, with delivery/payment information visible.

Do not make the homepage feel like a generic landing page. Make it a usable beauty shop from the first screen.

## Visual Details

Use thin gold dividers, delicate icons, subtle botanical/floral motifs, soft shadows, and plenty of whitespace. Keep the interface polished and readable. Avoid heavy gradients, neon colors, oversized rounded cards, cluttered decorative backgrounds, or anything that makes the shop feel cheap.

Photography direction: bright, close-up, clean product shots with real packaging visible. Use the product colors as small brand moments in each product card or collection page.

## Copy Direction

Tone should be elegant, helpful, and trustworthy. Use Bulgarian as the primary language for customer-facing text, with optional English/Russian product naming if it appears on packaging. Avoid exaggerated claims. Prefer clear phrases like:

- "Японска и азиатска козметика"
- "Премиално качество"
- "Натурални съставки"
- "Ефективни формули"
- "Ежедневна грижа"
- "Подбери грижа според нуждите на кожата"
