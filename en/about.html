An everyday ritual

How to use the masks

Four steps that take less than 20 minutes.

1

Cleanse

Wash your face and pat it dry with a soft towel.

2

Apply

Lay the mask over your face and smooth it flat against the skin.

3

Leave 10-15 min.

Relax. The essence sinks in and saturates the skin with moisture.

4

Pat in what is left

Do not rinse - gently pat the remaining essence into the skin.
