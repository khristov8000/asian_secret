Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are the products authentic?

Yes. Every product is authentic and imported from Japan, South Korea and Thailand in its original packaging and formulation.

How long will my order take?

We dispatch orders within 48 hours. The courier delivers in 1-3 working days depending on the town.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes, cash on delivery is our main payment method. Bank transfer is also possible - write to us for the details.

How do I choose a mask for my skin type?

In the catalogue you can filter by need: hydration, soothing, radiance, firmness, nourishment and protection. If you are unsure, write to us on Instagram and we will recommend one.

Can I return a product?

Yes, within 14 days with the packaging unopened, under the Bulgarian Consumer Protection Act. Please contact us first.
